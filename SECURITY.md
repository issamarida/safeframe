# Security Policy

SafeFrame handles sensitive photos and video of people who asked not to be
shown, many of them children. Please report vulnerabilities privately to
**issamaarida@gmail.com** rather than opening a public issue.

In scope, for example:

- uploads, previews or rendered files reachable by anyone other than the uploader
- files that survive deletion (uploads, previews, analysis data, outputs)
- path traversal or arbitrary file read/write through the API
- ways to make the pipeline silently skip blurring a band wearer
- ways to get a band honoured where it must not be: un-guarding a guarded
  zone, or an adult's band honoured in one
- ways to open, forge, truncate or re-target a sealed envelope without a
  trustee quorum, or to learn anything about its content from fewer shares
- ways to link sightings of a band, or to recover a capture location from
  any output, job file or log

Please do not include real footage of people in reports. You will receive an
acknowledgement within 72 hours.
